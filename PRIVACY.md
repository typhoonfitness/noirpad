# NoirPad — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 August 2026**

NoirPad collects nothing.

That is the whole policy, but the rest of this page spells out what that means
in practice, because "we don't collect data" is a claim worth being specific
about.

---

## What NoirPad does not do

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, no sign-in, and no user identity of any
  kind.
- **No analytics.** No usage tracking, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no
  session recording, no fingerprinting.
- **No advertising.** No ad networks, no ad identifiers, no marketing profiles.
- **No third-party SDKs.** The app is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks.
  Nothing else is linked in.
- **No network access.** NoirPad makes no network requests at all. It does not
  contact any server — mine or anyone else's. On macOS the app runs in Apple's
  sandbox without network permission, so it could not connect out even if it
  tried.
- **No tracking across apps or websites**, as that term is defined by Apple's
  App Tracking Transparency framework. NoirPad does not use that framework
  because it has nothing to ask permission for.

## What stays on your device

- **Your notes.** Stored in the app's own container on your device. They are not
  uploaded anywhere.
- **Your preferences.** Ink colour, font, text size, which effects are on, your
  word goal, and which note you had open. Stored in the standard system
  preferences store on your device.

Both are included in your device backup if you back up your device, because
that is how iOS and macOS treat app storage. Those backups belong to you and go
to your own iCloud account or your own computer, under Apple's terms, not mine.

Deleting NoirPad deletes both.

## Files you save and export

When you use **Open**, **Save** or **Export**, NoirPad uses the system file
picker. The app can only read or write the specific file you choose, and it has
no ability to browse your storage on its own. Where those files end up — iCloud
Drive, local storage, an external disk — is entirely your choice, and once
written they are ordinary files outside the app's control.

## Clipboard

The two "Copy to clipboard" export options put your text on the system
clipboard, and only when you tap them. NoirPad never reads the clipboard.

## Permissions

NoirPad requests no permissions. It does not use the camera, the microphone,
location, contacts, photos, calendars, health data, motion sensors, or
notifications. It plays audio out (the typewriter sounds), which needs no
permission, and it does so on the ambient channel so it mixes with whatever
else you are listening to.

## Children

NoirPad is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of NoirPad ever collects anything, this page will say so
before that version ships, and the change will be described plainly rather than
buried. The version history is public in the repository.
